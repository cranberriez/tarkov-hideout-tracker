# Battle-pass purchase planning

## Document route

In **Battle Pass → Docs Breakdown**, turn on **Optimizer** (off by default) to calculate routes and show claiming-profile recommendations on reward cards. Reward cards in the route show **Claim #N**, their position in the claim order, and **Claim suggested** claims those rewards on a page. With the optimizer off, no rewards are suggested. Then select a final-page reward, a saved goal, all saved goals, or **Finish the season**. In the required-rewards scope, finishing means claiming the final page and enough prerequisites to reach it. Select **All rewards through destination** with **Finish the season** to buy every outstanding reward. The controls recalculate the preview as inventory, costs, and selections change.

To see how far current inventory can take you, choose **Most rewards with owned documents**. Each new page requires all but one reward on the preceding page. Already claimed rewards count toward the gate across all profiles.

Each claim draws ordinary documents from one profile. Including all profiles lets the solver assign different claims to different inventories. A higher-priced prerequisite can be cheaper to fund when its document types match stock on hand. Turning on **Use classified** lets the solver spend the season’s shared classified balance after matching ordinary documents. The breakdown summarizes the configured route. Inventory coverage includes matching ordinary documents and any enabled owned classified documents. Each claim shows ordinary stock allocated to it after earlier claims, divided by its required quantity. The cash-free estimate finds the fewest full daily gathering allowances needed for that claim order using allowed documents, parallel profile caps, existing TarCoins and rewards earned before each purchase. **Classified to buy** follows the gathering target: classified documents still missing after owned ordinary and classified documents and the documents gathered, with the amount before gathering beneath it. A second summary row shows what is left after the whole route at the gathering target with the recommended purchase: **Classified documents left over** (owned classified the route does not use plus spare documents from packs) and **TarCoins left over**, with the cash paid and documents gathered. **Documents to gather**, below the summary tiles, shows one column per mode with the documents the gathering target needs, largest amounts first, and each mode's days. Collapsed, it shows each mode's total. When the route needs no cash, so there is no target to choose, it shows the cash-free plan instead.

### Gathering target

**Gathering target** in Route configuration chooses how much to gather: **No gathering** or one of the cash breakpoints, each the next drop in what you pay. The first options, the suggested route's entry and the chosen one always show; when more than two others remain, **More** adds them to the same list and **Less** after the last option collapses it again. Options are full-width selectable rows on a shared column grid (title with gather and swap lines, pay, save, TarCoins left) so figures line up; on narrow screens each row stacks. The choice drives the mode cards' **To gather**, the summary below them, the **Plan**. It is saved on this device; when the route changes and the saved amount is no longer a breakpoint, the recommended one is used.

### Season deadline

Gathering is capped at the whole days left in the season (`seasonDaysLeft` in [gathering-savings-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/gathering-savings-model.ts), from the season's `endsAt`): no mode gathers more than its daily cap times those days, and documents that don't fit are bought. The cap applies to the breakpoints, the selected target, mode previews, the map and document-type searches, claim previews and the cheapest-route comparison, so every option fits and its price is real. A saved target that needs more days stays saved: the picker shows it marked **Past season end**, and the plan gathers the last breakpoint (the most that fits) and buys the rest. The fastest cash-free finish is still solved without the cap; when it ends after the season, the summary tile is marked and a warning above the summary says how many days are left, what the target needs and what a cash-free finish needs. When Balanced would gather faster or pay less, the warning offers **Use Balanced**. Seasons without an end date have no cap.

### Preferred gathering mode

With **All profiles** on, **Route configuration** shows a card per mode under the heading **Preferred mode** with two lines for the gathering target as it would be with that mode preferred (the card's own when selected): the documents gathered in each mode, most first, which is where modes differ when every one finishes in a day, and the days, marked **late** when they pass the season end. Each card re-ranks the active route for its mode and prices it with the same number of gathered documents ([mode-preview.worker.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/mode-preview.worker.ts)). A mode turned off shows **Off**. Cards use the same lifted button style as the gathering target cards. The section is hidden by default and while calculating; it shows only once some mode would gather differently from the current one, since otherwise there is nothing to choose between. A saved preference still applies while it is hidden. Each mode's daily cap, owned documents and claims are shown in its **Documents to gather** column instead. Select a card to make that mode preferred; select it again to clear the preference. A fourth card, **Balanced**, instead moves claims between modes to shorten the slowest mode's gathering days at the saved gathering target (everything gatherable when none is saved), under the same free-move rules plus one more: after gathering the target, no claim needs classified earlier, so the checkout cannot rise. It is a local search that keeps a move when it shortens the slowest mode (whole days, then exact days) and keeps the result only when the slowest mode saves at least a whole day ([owner-preference-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/owner-preference-model.ts) `balanceOwners`). It runs in [owner-balance.worker.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/owner-balance.worker.ts) for the fewest-documents route and every alternative whenever several profiles plan, so the Balanced card can preview it; while Balanced is selected the route shows as calculating until the worker answers. Because it balances a target, changing the target can change the route and its breakpoints. After the route is solved, each claim moves to the preferred mode whenever that costs nothing: the same rewards and claim order, no classified document needed earlier and no claim lost from ready now. Gathering follows the claims, so the cash-free days change with the mode's daily cap. **Advanced: maps, modes and documents to gather** is always shown, with **Maps to farm** first, also when only one profile is included. It turns gathering off per mode or per document type; a mode turned off has its claims moved to other modes when that is free, and owned documents still count. These choices also drive the gathering target options. Next to **Fewest maps, same cost**, **Simplest** offers the fewest document types that still gather the whole target at no extra cash (`fewestTypes` in [map-filter-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/map-filter-model.ts), searched in a worker whenever the target or filters change). It only shows when it gathers fewer types than now; one click turns the other needed types off in every mode. The summary below the cards updates live. Model: [owner-preference-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/owner-preference-model.ts).

### Planned costs

**Use Planned Costs**, next to Use Defaults, switches reward cards from each reward's configured cost to what the active route spends on it: the owned and gathered documents it uses for the gathering target, plus a classified chip for the rest (owned or bought). Claimed rewards and rewards outside the route keep their configured cost. It needs the optimizer and a solved route. Model: `plannedSpend` in [claim-deduction-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/claim-deduction-model.ts).

### Reconfiguring a saved plan

**Reconfigure** reopens the configuration with the saved plan's settings. While the destination, rewards mode, all profiles, classified use, gathering target and the remaining claims with their owners and order all match the saved plan, **Replace saved plan** and **Cancel** become one **No changes, close** button, which returns to the saved plan without writing anything.

### Better route while a saved plan replays

While a saved plan replays, the route is solved afresh in the background with the plan's destination, rewards mode, profiles and classified use, on current inventory, classified balance and TarCoins (each change replaces the running solve). It gets the same preferred-owner reassignment as a new route. The fresh route and the replayed plan are priced at the plan's remaining gathering target, each gathering the fewest documents that reach its cash there. A banner at the top of Docs breakdown, above the saved plan, appears only when the fresh route pays less, pays the same with fewer documents to gather, or pays and gathers the same but leaves more TarCoins; different owners or order with the same outcome never show it. **Review** reconfigures at the fresh route's gathering target; the saved plan stays until replaced. **Dismiss** hides that suggestion on this device until the saved plan, inventories, classified balance, TarCoins or the suggestion itself change. Model: [better-route-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/better-route-model.ts).

### Claiming with a saved plan

While a saved plan replays, a reward's check mark, **Claim suggested** and **Claim all** open a confirmation instead of recording at once. It shows, per reward, the documents the plan expects you to hold (owned plus planned gathering) against the cost, and the classified documents that fill the rest. **Mine are different** turns each count into a stepper; the classified count follows. Rewards outside the plan use the selected inventory profile and what it owns. The confirmation replays the plan's remaining claims after these and reports how many more or fewer documents the rest needs and any change in checkout, compared with claiming the planned rewards as planned. Claiming is disabled while classified documents are short; the balance can be corrected in place. Confirming removes the documents; see [user state](user-state.md) for the writes and refunds.

## Funding the route

In **Route configuration**, enter the current global TarCoin balance and enable the one-time offer if it is still available. The breakdown stays hidden until a complete route can be calculated; funding and the plan are collapsed sections whose headers show the reward count, cash total and best saving. The cash recommendation lists the quantity, price per TarCoin pack, and subtotal for each pack size. The subtotals add up to the cash needed for the selected claim order. The schedule then shows how to spend those TarCoins on classified documents. Reward TarCoins become available after the corresponding claim. The optimizer finds the exact minimum cash cost, then recommends the most TarCoins within 10 cents of that amount. If the recommended purchase costs more, the receipt also shows the exact-minimum purchase and its ending balance. Gathering savings compare actual recommended purchase prices. The receipt and each gathering breakpoint also compare the cheapest higher-priced single bundles that provide more currency, showing the ending wallet for the same document purchases.

Recalculation is calm: values dim (no pulsing) while they update, new target rows fade in, the gathering target list holds its space with placeholder rows while calculating, and the Preferred mode section folds away after a short delay and unfolds without jumping what is below. Motion respects reduced-motion settings. Route configuration is deliberately terse: sections are headed **Route**, **Preferred mode**, **Gathering target** and **Advanced**, explanations live in tooltips, and cards show absolute figures. A gathering target card shows its title (with **· Alt N** and **Suggested route**), **Pay $X · save $Y** against paying with no gathering with the TarCoins left in aligned columns of one selectable row; the documents to gather per mode with days sit under the title. Differences against a plain neighbor only decide which alternatives show.

**Gathering target** in Route configuration lists every way gathering lowers the cash paid, so there is no separate savings section. Each option starts from current inventory and keeps the same rewards, claiming profiles, and order, and shows the cash paid, the saving and the documents per mode with the days they take. Under **Route configuration → Advanced: maps, modes and documents to gather**, turn off a mode or individual document types to recalculate gathering. Choices are saved on this device per profile, including each seasonal character. Turning a mode back on restores its document selections. Owned stock and reward claims still count in every mode. The list ends before gathering would fully cover the route. An option can still cost $0 when an existing TarCoin balance or reward income covers the remaining documents. **Route funding** shows the chosen target's purchase: TarCoins bought, the dollars paid, TarCoins left, the saving against not gathering and the pack receipt, plus a hint when farming a few TarCoins would lower the cash.

**Plan**, its own section below Route funding, merges the claim order with the buy timeline and also shows when nothing needs buying. Its header gives the reward count, how many are ready now and the purchase. Tiles show the cash paid, classified to buy, and the TarCoins and classified left. Below them, one row per action, top to bottom, just in time, with the TarCoins and classified documents (owned and bought) held after each row. Gather rows show document icons with counts per mode; buy rows show pack sizes as chips with their price in cash or TarCoins. Each claim row shows the reward, page, profile and **Ready now** when owned stock already covers it, then each document type as owned (+ gathered, tinted) of needed, the classified it uses and any reward TarCoins. If no purchase plan can be built, the claims still list in order. Classified purchases split the claims into runs. Before each run you gather that run's ordinary documents per mode, buy TarCoins only when the next classified pack is unaffordable, and buy classified packs only when a claim needs them. Packs keep the funding plan's order and are never bought earlier than planned, so reward TarCoins earned along the way always cover them. TarCoins the purchase adds beyond the claims appear last as **Buy TarCoins to keep**. It follows the gathering target and reprices the same claim order. Model: [purchase-flow-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/purchase-flow-model.ts).

**Leave 5 unplanned per day**, at the bottom of **Gathering target**, plans each mode 5 below its cap (PVP 20, PVE 10, seasonal 35). Near the limit, raids spawn far fewer documents, so the last few of an allowance are slow to find. The setting changes gathering days, not cost, and is saved on this device.

Each profile has a separate acquisition limit across all document types in a 24-hour period. PVP can collect 25, PVE can collect 15, and a seasonal character can collect 40. The estimate assumes the full allowance is available on each listed profile and counts periods in parallel. ## Alternative routes

Alternative claim routes appear as entries in the **Gathering target** list; there is no separate Route options section. Every route is priced at a gathering amount through `gatheringScenario` and the recommended TarCoin purchase, the same figure **Route funding** shows ([route-options-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/route-options-model.ts) `priceRoutes`). The default route is the cheapest checkout when every route gathers the same amount, the gathering target as it applies to the fewest-documents route (its breakpoints are kept once solved, so switching routes cannot move the comparison). Ties gather fewer documents, then leave more TarCoins, then keep the fewest-documents route. The entry for that default is marked **Suggested route**.

After the active route is calculated, a background worker solves the same route model again for each higher total of reward TarCoins. It keeps the fewest-document route at each level, buys at most 20 more classified documents than the active route and prefers the active route's rewards when choices tie. A route with more documents and no more reward TarCoins never costs less, so these levels cover every route worth comparing. Wallet and offer changes only reprice the routes; they do not re-solve.

Alternatives are numbered **Alt 1**, **Alt 2** in the list's order. An alternative entry is a card like any other (`Gather N · Alt 1`, or `No gathering · Alt 1`) with its swap as the last line, such as Pg.9: Black Division gear crate → TarCoin ×50. Its plain neighbor, the fewest-documents entry nearest in documents gathered (No gathering for the alternative's own no-gathering entry), decides whether it shows: an entry equal to its neighbor in cash, documents and TarCoins is hidden. The route planner minimizes documents first, so an alternative with one more document can still win when it earns reward TarCoins. Selecting an entry adopts its route and sets the gathering target together; claim markers, claim order, funding, gathering and the step plan follow it. The pick is not saved beyond the gathering target: it ends when any route setting changes or the page reloads, and the saved target then follows the usual fallback.

Alternative entries are computed on the main thread, after render, without a worker per alternative ([alternate-entries-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/alternate-entries-model.ts)). For each alternative the model prices the fewest-documents route's breakpoint counts (and that count plus the alternative's extra documents), then binary-searches the minimum count that reaches the same cash, so an entry's amount is a true breakpoint of its route and the active route's worker returns the same cash. An entry shows only when no fewest-documents point costs as much or less, gathers as few documents and keeps as many TarCoins (cheaper cash, the same cash with fewer documents, or the same cash with more TarCoins left, such as a swap one document later that earns reward TarCoins); entries beaten the same way by another alternative are dropped and at most eight remain. By default the list shows the fewest-documents entries (the first two, the suggested and the chosen one) and only the best alternative's entries beside them; the best has the largest saving against its plain neighbors, ties gathering fewer documents, then keeping more TarCoins. The other alternatives sit behind **More options**. **No gathering** always selects the fewest-documents route without gathering; the swap's own no-gathering entry is separate. Alternatives receive the same preferred-owner reassignment as the fewest-documents route before they are priced. Previous entries stay on screen while they recalculate.

## Planning pipeline

One owner, [useBattlePassPlan](../src/features/battle-pass/useBattlePassPlan.ts), runs each step once and provides the results through `BattlePassPlanContext`; the Docs breakdown panels and the reward cards' claim markers only read them:

1. Season data, configured costs, inventories, progress and route settings (session only) build the route request.
2. A HiGHS worker (`public/battle-pass/route.mjs`) solves the route; owner preference ([owner-preference-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/owner-preference-model.ts)) reassigns claims, with Balanced in its own worker.
3. Coin-level solves in the same worker give alternative routes, which receive the same owner preference. Route options price every route with the funding settings; an adopted alternative becomes the active route.
4. Funding is solved once for the active route.
5. A gathering worker ([gathering-savings.worker.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/gathering-savings.worker.ts)) computes the breakpoints, capped at the days left in the season, and the fastest cash-free plan. Previous results stay on screen, marked as syncing, while it recalculates.
6. The selected gathering target and its purchase schedule feed the step-by-step flow ([purchase-flow-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/purchase-flow-model.ts)).

Settings are read and written through `useFundingSettings` as before; the pipeline saves nothing itself.
