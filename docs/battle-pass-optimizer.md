# Battle-pass purchase planning

## Document route

In **Battle Pass → Docs Breakdown**, turn on **Optimizer** (off by default) to calculate routes and show claiming-profile recommendations on reward cards. Reward cards in the route show **Claim #N**, their position in the claim order, and **Claim suggested** claims those rewards on a page. With the optimizer off, no rewards are suggested. Then select a final-page reward, a saved goal, all saved goals, or **Finish the season**. In the required-rewards scope, finishing means claiming the final page and enough prerequisites to reach it. Select **All rewards through destination** with **Finish the season** to buy every outstanding reward. The controls recalculate the preview as inventory, costs, and selections change.

To see how far current inventory can take you, choose **Most rewards with owned documents**. Each new page requires all but one reward on the preceding page. Already claimed rewards count toward the gate across all profiles.

Each claim draws ordinary documents from one profile. Including all profiles lets the solver assign different claims to different inventories. A higher-priced prerequisite can be cheaper to fund when its document types match stock on hand. Turning on **Use classified** lets the solver spend the season’s shared classified balance after matching ordinary documents. The breakdown summarizes the configured route. Inventory coverage includes matching ordinary documents and any enabled owned classified documents. Each claim shows ordinary stock allocated to it after earlier claims, divided by its required quantity. The cash-free estimate finds the fewest full daily gathering allowances needed for that claim order using allowed documents, parallel profile caps, existing TarCoins and rewards earned before each purchase. **Classified to buy** follows the gathering target: classified documents still missing after owned ordinary and classified documents and the documents gathered, with the amount before gathering beneath it. A second summary row shows what is left after the whole route at the gathering target with the recommended purchase: **Classified documents left over** (owned classified the route does not use plus spare documents from packs) and **TarCoins left over**, with the cash paid and documents gathered. **Documents to gather**, below the summary tiles, shows one column per mode with the documents the gathering target needs, largest amounts first, and each mode's days. Collapsed, it shows each mode's total. When the route needs no cash, so there is no target to choose, it shows the cash-free plan instead.

### Gathering target

**Gathering target** in Route configuration chooses how much to gather: **No gathering** or one of the breakpoints from **Save cash by gathering**, each the next drop in what you pay. The first options and the recommended one always show; when more than two others remain, **More** adds them to the same list and **Less** after the last option collapses it again. Options fill two columns on narrow screens and four on wide ones. The choice drives the mode cards' **To gather**, the summary below them, the highlighted option in **Save cash by gathering** and the **Step-by-step plan**. It is saved on this device; when the route changes and the saved amount is no longer a breakpoint, the recommended one is used.

### Season deadline

Gathering is capped at the whole days left in the season (`seasonDaysLeft` in [gathering-savings-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/gathering-savings-model.ts), from the season's `endsAt`): no mode gathers more than its daily cap times those days, and documents that don't fit are bought. The cap applies to the breakpoints, the selected target, mode previews, the map and document-type searches, claim previews and the cheapest-route comparison, so every option fits and its price is real. A saved target that needs more days stays saved: the picker shows it marked **Past season end**, and the plan gathers the last breakpoint (the most that fits) and buys the rest. The fastest cash-free finish is still solved without the cap; when it ends after the season, the summary tile is marked and a warning above the summary says how many days are left, what the target needs and what a cash-free finish needs. When Balanced would gather faster or pay less, the warning offers **Use Balanced**. Seasons without an end date have no cap.

### Preferred gathering mode

With **All profiles** on, **Route configuration** shows a card per mode with what switching the preferred mode to it would change, compared with the current choice: gathering days at the gathering target, days to the fastest cash-free finish (solved in [mode-preview.worker.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/mode-preview.worker.ts)), how many claims change mode, and checkout. Each card re-ranks the active route for its mode and prices it with the same number of gathered documents; the current card shows its own gathering and cash-free days. Each mode's daily cap, owned documents and claims are shown in its **Documents to gather** column instead. Select a card to make that mode preferred; select it again to clear the preference. Cash-free days past the season end are marked **late**. A fourth card, **Balanced**, instead moves claims between modes to shorten the slowest mode's gathering days at the saved gathering target (everything gatherable when none is saved), under the same free-move rules plus one more: after gathering the target, no claim needs classified earlier, so the checkout cannot rise. It is a local search that keeps a move when it shortens the slowest mode (whole days, then exact days) and keeps the result only when the slowest mode saves at least a whole day ([owner-preference-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/owner-preference-model.ts) `balanceOwners`). It runs in [owner-balance.worker.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/owner-balance.worker.ts) for the fewest-documents route and every alternative whenever several profiles plan, so the Balanced card can preview it; while Balanced is selected the route shows as calculating until the worker answers. Because it balances a target, changing the target can change the route and its breakpoints. After the route is solved, each claim moves to the preferred mode whenever that costs nothing: the same rewards and claim order, no classified document needed earlier and no claim lost from ready now. Gathering follows the claims, so the cash-free days change with the mode's daily cap. **Advanced: maps, modes and documents to gather** is always shown, with **Maps to farm** first, also when only one profile is included. It turns gathering off per mode or per document type; a mode turned off has its claims moved to other modes when that is free, and owned documents still count. These choices also drive **Save cash by gathering**. Next to **Fewest maps, same cost**, **Simplest** offers the fewest document types that still gather the whole target at no extra cash (`fewestTypes` in [map-filter-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/map-filter-model.ts), searched in a worker whenever the target or filters change). It only shows when it gathers fewer types than now; one click turns the other needed types off in every mode. The summary below the cards updates live. Model: [owner-preference-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/owner-preference-model.ts).

### Planned costs

**Use Planned Costs**, next to Use Defaults, switches reward cards from each reward's configured cost to what the active route spends on it: the owned and gathered documents it uses for the gathering target, plus a classified chip for the rest (owned or bought). Claimed rewards and rewards outside the route keep their configured cost. It needs the optimizer and a solved route. Model: `plannedSpend` in [claim-deduction-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/claim-deduction-model.ts).

### Reconfiguring a saved plan

**Reconfigure** reopens the configuration with the saved plan's settings. While the destination, rewards mode, all profiles, classified use, gathering target and the remaining claims with their owners and order all match the saved plan, **Replace saved plan** and **Cancel** become one **No changes, close** button, which returns to the saved plan without writing anything.

### Claiming with a saved plan

While a saved plan replays, a reward's check mark, **Claim suggested** and **Claim all** open a confirmation instead of recording at once. It shows, per reward, the documents the plan expects you to hold (owned plus planned gathering) against the cost, and the classified documents that fill the rest. **Mine are different** turns each count into a stepper; the classified count follows. Rewards outside the plan use the selected inventory profile and what it owns. The confirmation replays the plan's remaining claims after these and reports how many more or fewer documents the rest needs and any change in checkout, compared with claiming the planned rewards as planned. Claiming is disabled while classified documents are short; the balance can be corrected in place. Confirming removes the documents; see [user state](user-state.md) for the writes and refunds.

## Funding the route

In **Route configuration**, enter the current global TarCoin balance and enable the one-time offer if it is still available. The breakdown stays hidden until a complete route can be calculated; claim order, funding and gathering savings are collapsed sections whose headers show the reward count, cash total and best saving. The cash recommendation lists the quantity, price per TarCoin pack, and subtotal for each pack size. The subtotals add up to the cash needed for the selected claim order. The schedule then shows how to spend those TarCoins on classified documents. Reward TarCoins become available after the corresponding claim. The optimizer finds the exact minimum cash cost, then recommends the most TarCoins within 10 cents of that amount. If the recommended purchase costs more, the receipt also shows the exact-minimum purchase and its ending balance. Gathering savings compare actual recommended purchase prices. The receipt and each gathering breakpoint also compare the cheapest higher-priced single bundles that provide more currency, showing the ending wallet for the same document purchases.

**Save cash by gathering** shows how additional ordinary documents would change that receipt. Each alternative starts from current inventory and keeps the same rewards, claiming profiles, and order. The highlighted recommendation lists quantities by mode. Under **Route configuration → Advanced: maps, modes and documents to gather**, turn off a mode or individual document types to recalculate gathering. Choices are saved on this device per profile, including each seasonal character. Turning a mode back on restores its document selections. Owned stock and reward claims still count in every mode. Gather the cumulative quantities in a single row to reach its cash total. The list ends before gathering would fully cover the route. A row can still cost $0 when an existing TarCoin balance or reward income covers the remaining documents.

**Step-by-step plan**, its own section below Route funding and the gathering savings, also shows when nothing needs buying. It lays the active route out left to right, just in time. Classified purchases split the claims into runs. Before each run you gather that run's ordinary documents per mode, buy TarCoins only when the next classified pack is unaffordable, and buy classified packs only when a claim needs them. Packs keep the funding plan's order and are never bought earlier than planned, so reward TarCoins earned along the way always cover them. TarCoins the purchase adds beyond the claims appear last as **Buy TarCoins to keep**. It follows the gathering target and reprices the same claim order. Model: [purchase-flow-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/purchase-flow-model.ts).

**Leave 5 unplanned per day**, below the preferred gathering mode cards, plans each mode 5 below its cap (PVP 20, PVE 10, seasonal 35). Near the limit, raids spawn far fewer documents, so the last few of an allowance are slow to find. The setting changes gathering days, not cost, and is saved on this device.

Each profile has a separate acquisition limit across all document types in a 24-hour period. PVP can collect 25, PVE can collect 15, and a seasonal character can collect 40. The estimate assumes the full allowance is available on each listed profile and counts periods in parallel. Edit these values in [gathering-caps.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/gathering-caps.ts).

## Route options

**Route options** compares viable claim routes and TarCoin purchases. The active route is chosen from them: each route is priced with the same number of gathered documents, the gathering target as it applies to the fewest-documents route (its breakpoints are kept once solved, so switching routes cannot move the comparison), and the cheapest checkout wins. Ties gather fewer documents, then leave more TarCoins, then keep the fewest-documents route. Until the alternatives and the fewest-documents route's gathering are solved, the fewest-documents route is shown. The winning card is marked **Lowest checkout**. The route planner minimizes documents first, so a route with one more document can still be better when it earns reward TarCoins. For example, on page 08 TarCoin ×50 costs one more document than White accent walls. Depending on the wallet and inventory, that swap can avoid a larger pack, leave more TarCoins for the same checkout or cost more.

After the active route is calculated, a background worker solves the same route model again for each higher total of reward TarCoins. It keeps the fewest-document route at each level, buys at most 20 more classified documents than the active route and prefers the active route's rewards when choices tie. A route with more documents and no more reward TarCoins never costs less, so these levels cover every route worth comparing. Wallet and offer changes only reprice the routes; they do not re-solve.

Each route lists TarCoin purchases up to $10 above its minimum that leave more TarCoins after the pass. **Show more options** under a route adds one purchase: the cheapest single regular pack (with the one-time offer when it is enabled) that funds the route and costs more than the listed purchases. **Show fewer** hides it again, and it resets when the routes are recalculated. Purchases within 10¢ of each other form a price tier, which keeps the one leaving the most TarCoins; the cheapest tier also keeps the exact minimum. An option is hidden when another option costs no more, buys no more classified documents and leaves at least as many TarCoins; the active route always keeps its cheapest and recommended purchases, so its card matches Route funding. The section shows one card per route: the fewest-documents route first, then each alternative with its reward swaps and classified documents to buy. Each card lists its purchases with the pack receipt, the cash paid now and the TarCoins left. From the second purchase on, **per extra $1** shows the extra TarCoins left for each extra dollar compared with the purchase above it on the same card (none within the same 10¢ price tier). Each purchase states the difference from the cheapest checkout of the fewest-documents route, such as “Spend $0.01 more and keep 100 more TC”. The active route's recommended purchase is marked as the one shown in Route funding.

Each route card is collapsed to a summary: its reward swaps, classified documents to buy, the cheapest checkout with TarCoins left and the most TarCoins it can leave. **Details** opens the rest. Inside, the card first shows its **Classified documents**: the document packs each listed purchase leads to, the total TarCoins they cost, spare documents and when to buy each pack in the claim order. It compares them with the cheapest packs if every TarCoin were available up front; a difference means the purchase must wait for TarCoins earned along the claim order. Purchases that share the same packs and timing share one entry. The TarCoin purchases follow. The section sits above Route funding because the claim order does not depend on how TarCoins are bought.

Alternatives receive the same preferred-owner reassignment as the fewest-documents route before they are priced. **Use this route** overrides that choice: claim markers, claim order, funding, gathering and the step plan follow the picked route, and **Use the cheapest route** returns to the automatic choice. The pick is not saved: it ends when any route setting changes or the page reloads. Model: [route-options-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/route-options-model.ts).

## Planning pipeline

One owner, [useBattlePassPlan](../src/features/battle-pass/useBattlePassPlan.ts), runs each step once and provides the results through `BattlePassPlanContext`; the Docs breakdown panels and the reward cards' claim markers only read them:

1. Season data, configured costs, inventories, progress and route settings (session only) build the route request.
2. A HiGHS worker (`public/battle-pass/route.mjs`) solves the route; owner preference ([owner-preference-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/owner-preference-model.ts)) reassigns claims, with Balanced in its own worker.
3. Coin-level solves in the same worker give alternative routes, which receive the same owner preference. Route options price every route with the funding settings; an adopted alternative becomes the active route.
4. Funding is solved once for the active route.
5. A gathering worker ([gathering-savings.worker.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/gathering-savings.worker.ts)) computes the breakpoints, capped at the days left in the season, and the fastest cash-free plan. Previous results stay on screen, marked as syncing, while it recalculates.
6. The selected gathering target and its purchase schedule feed the step-by-step flow ([purchase-flow-model.ts](../src/features/battle-pass/lib/purchase-flow-model.ts)).

Settings are read and written through `useFundingSettings` as before; the pipeline saves nothing itself.
