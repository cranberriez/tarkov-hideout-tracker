# Map card artwork

Location banners from the [Escape from Tarkov Wiki](https://escapefromtarkov.fandom.com/wiki/Map_of_Tarkov).
Game artwork © Battlestate Games and its licensors. Wiki community content is
provided under [CC BY-NC-SA](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)
unless otherwise noted; original rights remain with their respective owners.

Exact download URLs and source pages are recorded in [sources.json](sources.json).
The Lab banner comes from [MohiKov's credited wiki mirror](https://mohilabs.com/mohikov/legacy-app).
The Lab (Dark) intentionally shares the Lab thumbnail; no separate dark variant
is claimed. Factory and Ground Zero variants use their workspace map-group artwork.

Downloaded 2026-10-06. Converted to WebP quality 72, resized/cropped to at most
480 × 288 without upscaling (the mirrored Lab source is 300 × 181). These are
decorative selection previews, not navigation maps. Keep image download and
conversion out of runtime and release generation.
